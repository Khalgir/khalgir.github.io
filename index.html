Channel Uploader

Channel Uploader is a private tool used by its owner, Levent Sevenler, to publish videos he makes to his own YouTube channels and to read those channels' statistics. The channels are Histories 'n Mysteries, Frightmare Studios, NextPillar and Aktüel Akıl.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users. It runs on the owner's own computer.
